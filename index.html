    Troubleshooting

    Can't find your name, or the result doesn't look right? It's usually one of these:

    • You're not in the Piano Piano Strava club. Only club members' rides are included, so riders outside the club won't show up.
    • You haven't ridden a Phoenix Park loop at high effort. Everything is based on your best lap of the 9.2 km loop. If you've only ridden it at an easy pace, your best lap understates what you can do, and rides will look harder than they are.
